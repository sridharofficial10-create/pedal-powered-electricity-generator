# Pedal-Powered Electricity Generator

## About the Project

This project is a simple pedal-powered electricity generation system that converts human pedaling effort into electrical energy.

The main idea is to use the mechanical energy produced while pedaling and convert it into electrical energy that can be used for small electrical applications.

## Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate a simple and sustainable method of generating electricity using human effort.

It also helps to understand the basic relationship between mechanical motion and electrical energy generation.

## How It Works

When the user pedals the system, the rotating motion is transferred to a generator.

The generator converts this mechanical rotation into electrical energy. The generated electricity can then be used to power or charge suitable low-power electrical devices.

### Basic Working

**Pedaling → Mechanical Rotation → Generator → Electrical Energy → Load**

The prototype was tested by operating the pedal mechanism and observing the electrical output produced by the generator.

Different operating conditions were considered to understand how pedaling affects the generated electrical energy.

The testing results and project images are included in this repository.
